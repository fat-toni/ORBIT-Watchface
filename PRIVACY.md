# 🔒 Privacy Policy – ORBIT

Last updated: 2026-10-26

ORBIT is a watchface for Amazfit watches with Zepp OS. It is free, open source and made by one hobby developer.
**ORBIT does not collect, save, send or share any data.**

## What the watchface uses

Everything below is read on the watch, shown on the watchface and nowhere else. Nothing is saved or sent.
A value is only read when you show it in one of the rings.

| Permission | What for |
|---|---|
| Steps (`data:user.hd.step`) | Steps ring and its goal |
| Calories (`data:user.hd.calorie`) | Calories ring and its goal |
| Heart rate (`data:user.hd.heart_rate`) | Heart-rate ring |
| Distance (`data:user.hd.distance`) | Distance ring |
| PAI (`data:user.hd.pai`) | PAI ring |
| Stand (`data:user.hd.stand`) | Stand ring and its goal |
| Blood oxygen (`data:user.hd.spo2`) | SpO₂ ring |
| Stress (`data:user.hd.stress`) | Stress ring |
| User info (`data:user.info`) | Your age, for the maximum heart rate (220 minus age) that fills the heart-rate ring |

The weather and the battery level come from the watch itself. The choices you make in the
watchface editor are kept by the watch.

ORBIT has no internet access, no phone-side part, no account, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs.

## Children

The watchface collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
